COMMON CONCERNS

Questions that shouldn’t need an email

Short, direct answers based on Red Shed’s current Learn to Row information.

🚣

“I’ve never rowed.”

That is exactly what Learn to Row is for. No rowing experience is needed and skills are taught from the beginning.

💪

“I’m not fit enough.”

You do not need to be fit before starting. The program builds fitness gradually while you learn.

🏊

“I’m worried about swimming.”

Red Shed asks rowers to be able to swim 50 metres and tread water. If that is a concern, talk to Red Shed before booking.

🌤️

“Am I too old?”

No. Adult programs regularly include rowers in their 50s, 60s and beyond.

🗓️

“Which course fits my week?”

Adult Learn to Row can run as weekend term programs or shorter early-morning intensives. Current listings show the exact days, times and price.

🔙

“I rowed years ago.”

You can start fresh with Learn to Row, or use a continuing pathway if your previous experience still gives you a comfortable base.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Your first session, without the mystery

Arrive about 15 minutes early. Your first session starts with the essentials on land: boats and equipment, a safety briefing and first strokes on the rowing machine before heading onto the water.

Boats, oars, safety gear and coaching are provided. Bring fitted activewear, socks, a water bottle, sun protection and a warm layer in cooler weather.
