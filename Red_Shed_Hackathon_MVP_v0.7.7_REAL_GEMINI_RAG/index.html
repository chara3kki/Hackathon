COMMON QUESTIONS, WITHOUT THE EMAIL LOOP

I've never rowed. Where do I start?+

Use Find My Rowing Path or ask the AI guide. It will use the current official Red Shed pages to point you toward the most relevant starting option.

I rowed years ago. Does my experience still count?+

This was a repeated theme in Red Shed's enquiry research. The AI guide can compare the current Learn to Row and Continue to Row information rather than relying on an old fixed answer.

Can the bot answer questions outside Learn to Row?+

Yes. It searches discoverable public content across the Red Shed website, including other Red Shed program and information pages.

What happens when Red Shed changes something?+

The website index refreshes automatically while the server is running, and can also be refreshed manually from the AI drawer.

Will it invent a price, course date or available place?+

No. The AI is instructed to use official retrieved Red Shed material and avoid inventing live prices, availability, policies or dates. Where needed, it sends the visitor to the relevant official page.
